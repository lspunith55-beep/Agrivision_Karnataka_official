SHARED-LOAD OPPORTUNITY

One journey can potentially serve multiple compatible orders.

This is a central AgriVision logistics concept. If your accepted movement has unused capacity and another order can reasonably fit the route, the Smart Route system can propose a shared movement. The participating orders can then receive an optimized transportation allocation instead of requiring completely independent journeys.

AGRIVISION DRIVER PRINCIPLE

The best delivery is one that makes sense for the whole network.

A driver should be able to earn from useful work while farmers get dependable movement, buyers receive their orders, vehicles use their capacity effectively and the logistics network avoids unnecessary journeys. That is why delivery matching, Smart Route, tracking and completion are designed as one connected workflow.
