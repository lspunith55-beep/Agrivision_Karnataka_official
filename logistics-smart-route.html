Don't Just Find A Route. Optimize The Journey.

AgriVision Smart Route Optimization looks beyond the shortest road. It evaluates whether accepted agricultural orders can share available vehicle capacity, whether their pickup and delivery requirements are compatible, what movement sequence is practical, what the combined transportation requirement costs, and how that cost can be fairly allocated between participating orders.

THE CORE AGRIVISION IDEA

Turn unused vehicle capacity into shared value.

Imagine one accepted order from Bengaluru to Chitradurga with an example transportation cost of ₹100. Another accepted order may need transportation from Tumakuru to Hiriyur with an example cost of ₹50. If a suitable vehicle has available capacity and the two movements can actually be combined, AgriVision should evaluate the shared journey instead of automatically treating the orders as two completely independent trips.

FAIR COST ALLOCATION

Shared transportation should create savings without hiding the calculation.

AgriVision should show the difference between the cost of separate movements and the cost of a feasible shared movement. The final allocated cost for each order should be based on the contribution and characteristics of that order—not on an arbitrary fixed split.
