WHY REQUESTS MATTER

Demand becomes useful when it has enough context.

A simple statement such as “I need vegetables” does not provide enough information for a useful agricultural transaction. A structured request can communicate exactly what the Buyer is looking for and provide the Farmer with a clearer understanding of the requirement.

CREATE REQUEST

Tell Farmers exactly what you are looking for.

Complete the request with practical information. If you discovered a suitable listing in the marketplace, include its reference so the requirement can remain connected to the original agricultural opportunity.

WHAT HAPPENS NEXT

Your request starts a conversation.

Submitting a request does not mean that an order has already been created. It creates a structured expression of demand that can move through the next stages of the Buyer-Farmer workflow.

A request expresses demand. Negotiation creates agreement.

Once a suitable Farmer and requirement are connected, the next stage is to understand the commercial terms. Quantity, price, timing and other requirements may need to be discussed before both sides are ready for a confirmed order. That is why the next Buyer stage is designed around orders and negotiation.
