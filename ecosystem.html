Seven Core Systems

Every participant has a place. Every role contributes to the ecosystem.

Farmer

The heart of the agricultural ecosystem. Farmers can connect with buyers, access agricultural services, discover resources and participate in a connected digital farming environment.

Explore Farmer

Buyer

A dedicated system connecting buyers with agricultural produce and helping create a more direct and transparent connection between agricultural supply and demand.

Explore Buyer

Logistics

The movement layer of the ecosystem, supporting the coordination of agricultural transportation and helping connect produce with the places where it needs to go.

Explore Logistics

Driver

Drivers form the operational connection between transportation requirements and agricultural movement, helping ensure goods can move efficiently through the ecosystem.

Explore Driver

Agricultural Labour

Connecting agricultural workers with opportunities and helping farms find the human resources required for different agricultural activities.

Explore Labour

Equipment Owner

A system for agricultural machinery and equipment owners to make their resources discoverable and connect them with farmers and agricultural requirements.

Explore Equipment

Operator

Skilled machine operators form the operational bridge between agricultural machinery and field requirements, helping equipment become useful on the ground.

Explore Operator

How the Ecosystem Connects

Agriculture becomes stronger when its participants work as one connected system.

Farm

Agricultural requirements and opportunities originate at the farm level.

People

Farmers, workers, operators and other participants become connected.

Resources

Machinery, equipment, skills and services become accessible through the ecosystem.

Movement

Logistics and drivers help agricultural goods and resources move where needed.

Market

Buyers connect with agricultural supply, completing the ecosystem cycle.

What Makes the Ecosystem Different

Designed around connection, accessibility and participation.

Connected

Instead of treating agricultural participants as isolated groups, AgriVision brings them together inside one ecosystem.

Inclusive

Farmers, buyers, workers, drivers, logistics providers, equipment owners and operators each have a dedicated role.

Efficient

The ecosystem is designed to reduce unnecessary separation between people, agricultural requirements and available resources.

Structured

Each core system has its own purpose, while remaining part of the larger AgriVision ecosystem.

Digital

The ecosystem provides a digital foundation through which agricultural participants can discover and connect with relevant opportunities and services.

Future Ready

A connected foundation can support the evolution of agricultural services, technology, machinery, transportation and markets.
