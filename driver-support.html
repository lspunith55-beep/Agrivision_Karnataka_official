COMMON QUESTIONS

The answers you may need while working.

What if my route changes?

Open the Smart Route or active Trip workspace, review the proposed movement and record an exception if the planned route is no longer practical.

What if I have extra vehicle capacity?

Use Smart Route to look for compatible orders rather than independently adding cargo to an active movement.

What if a payment looks incorrect?

Review the earnings record first, then create a payment-related support query with the relevant shipment information.

What if a document needs attention?

Open the Documents workspace to review the record, expiry information and current verification state.

What if cargo has an issue?

Record the condition through the relevant trip workflow and create a support request when additional assistance is required.

Why should I report delays?

Accurate status information allows connected participants and Logistics to work from the same operational picture.

AGRIVISION PRINCIPLE

Good communication keeps a complicated agricultural network understandable.

Farmers, Buyers, Drivers, Logistics, Labour, Equipment Owners, Operators and other participants may all depend on the same movement. A small change in one place can affect another participant. That is why AgriVision treats communication and operational reporting as part of the logistics system itself.
