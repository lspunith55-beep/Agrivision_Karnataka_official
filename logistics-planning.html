WHY PLANNING MATTERS

A route is only useful when the whole journey is feasible.

The shortest route is not automatically the best agricultural route. A practical plan must consider whether the vehicle has sufficient capacity, whether the driver is available, whether the pickup and delivery windows can be respected, whether the cargo requires special handling and whether other compatible orders can use available capacity.

AGRIVISION INTELLIGENCE

One vehicle can sometimes serve more than one accepted order. The system must prove that it is feasible.

Planning prepares the information required for that decision. For example, if one accepted movement is Bengaluru → Chitradurga and another accepted movement is Tumakuru → Hiriyur, AgriVision should not automatically combine them. It should first evaluate the available vehicle capacity, pickup sequence, delivery windows, route compatibility, cargo requirements and additional movement cost.
