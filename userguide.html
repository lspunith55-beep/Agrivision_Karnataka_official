02 / UNDERSTAND

What exactly is AgriVision?

AgriVision Karnataka is designed as a connected agricultural ecosystem. Instead of treating farming, market access, labour, machinery and transportation as completely isolated services, the system is structured around the relationships between them.

Agriculture is not a single activity.

It is a connected journey.
A farmer may need a buyer.

A crop can create a market requirement and connect the farmer with procurement activity.

A farm may need people.

Agricultural work can create requirements for labour and machine operators.

A farm may need resources.

Equipment and machinery can become part of the agricultural workflow.

A crop may need transportation.

Logistics providers, vehicles and drivers can become part of the journey from farm to buyer.

04 / YOUR ROLE

Choose where you participate.

Every role has a different responsibility, a different view of the ecosystem and different connections. Select the role that best represents how you participate in agriculture.

01

Farmer

Agricultural production Read Farmer Guide

What it is

The Farmer module is centred around agricultural production and farm management.

How to use it

Connect farm information, crops, listings, buyers, labour, equipment, operators and logistics around your agricultural requirements.

Your advantage

A farmer-centred workspace can bring different parts of the agricultural journey into one connected environment.

02

Buyer

Agricultural procurement Read Buyer Guide

What it is

The Buyer module represents agricultural demand and procurement requirements.

How to use it

Discover relevant crops, create requirements, connect with farmers, coordinate orders and follow delivery.

Your advantage

Procurement can be connected with the agricultural source and the delivery journey instead of remaining isolated.

03

Labour Worker

Agricultural workforce Read Labour Guide

What it is

The Labour module focuses on agricultural workforce coordination.

How to use it

Maintain relevant information, availability and connections with agricultural work requirements.

Your advantage

Agricultural work requirements can be connected with available workforce participation.

04

Logistics Provider

Transportation coordination Read Logistics Guide

What it is

The Logistics module coordinates agricultural transportation and fleet operations.

How to use it

Coordinate farmer, buyer, vehicle, driver, route and delivery requirements.

Your advantage

Transportation becomes part of the agricultural workflow instead of an unrelated external step.

05

Driver

Agricultural transportation Read Driver Guide

What it is

The Driver module focuses on the actual movement of agricultural deliveries.

How to use it

Review delivery information, respond to assignments and follow the delivery journey through completion.

Your advantage

You receive information relevant to the delivery you are responsible for rather than the entire ecosystem.

06

Equipment Owner

Agricultural machinery Read Equipment Guide

What it is

The Equipment Owner module manages agricultural machinery availability and coordination.

How to use it

Maintain equipment information and coordinate availability around relevant agricultural requirements.

Your advantage

Agricultural equipment can become more visible within the ecosystem when it is available for coordination.

07

Machine Operator

Machinery operation Read Operator Guide

What it is

The Machine Operator role represents people who operate agricultural machinery.

How to use it

Represent your capabilities and connect them with relevant machinery and agricultural requirements.

Your advantage

Equipment ownership and machine operation remain separate roles while still being connected.

11 / THE DIFFERENCE

From disconnected services to connected agriculture.

Fragmented approach

Different needs. Different places.

Farmer and buyer may operate separately.
Labour requirements can remain separate from workforce information.
Equipment and operators can remain disconnected.
Transportation can sit outside the agricultural workflow.
Ecosystem approach

Different roles. One connected journey.

Seven roles participate inside one ecosystem.
Agricultural activities can connect relevant participants.
Shared records can be represented through different role perspectives.
Market, resources and logistics can form part of one agricultural journey.
