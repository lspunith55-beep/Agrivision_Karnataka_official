PICKUP

Start the movement by confirming the pickup.

The Driver should confirm arrival at the pickup location before loading begins. This creates a reliable operational timestamp and keeps the Farmer and Logistics records synchronized.

LOADING

Record what actually enters the vehicle.

Loading is connected to cargo, capacity and any supporting labour or equipment. If a shared load has been accepted, the system must account for all participating cargo before the vehicle departs.

INTERMEDIATE MOVEMENT

Shared journeys may have multiple stops. Keep every stop visible.

When Smart Route combines compatible orders, the vehicle may need to visit additional pickup or delivery locations. These stops must be explicitly represented so that the driver knows what happens next and the connected orders remain synchronized.

EXCEPTION REPORTING

Real roads change. Record what happened.

Traffic, road conditions, weather, vehicle problems and loading delays can change the expected journey. The Driver should be able to report these events so Logistics and connected participants are not working with outdated assumptions.
