LABOUR • TEAM WORKSPACE

Many hands. One coordinated team.

Agricultural operations often require several workers at the same time. Instead of making a Farmer find every Labourer separately, AgriVision can support coordinated Labour teams. A team can maintain its members, skills, availability, work commitments and performance while Farmers can request the workforce required for a particular operation.
