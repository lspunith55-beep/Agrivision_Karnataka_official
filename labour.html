WHAT IS IT?

The Human Workforce Layer of Agriculture

Agriculture depends on people. Cultivation, harvesting, field work, maintenance and many other activities require workers with different skills and availability. The Labour Module brings this human workforce into the connected AgriVision ecosystem.

Represent agricultural workers, their skills, availability and work-related information.

THE CHALLENGE

Agricultural Work Needs the Right People

Farms may require workers at specific times for specific agricultural activities, while workers need opportunities that match their availability, skills and location.

Skills

LABOUR WORKFLOW

Requirement → Match → Work

The Labour Module creates a connected path between agricultural labour demand and workers available to perform the required activities.

01 — Requirement

A farm identifies agricultural work that needs to be performed.

02 — Discover

Relevant workers and agricultural work opportunities can be discovered.

03 — Match

Consider skills, availability and location when connecting demand and workers.

04 — Connect

Workers and farms communicate around the agricultural work requirement.

05 — Schedule

Organize when and where the agricultural work will take place.

06 — Complete

The agricultural task progresses toward completion.
