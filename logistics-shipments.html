A shipment is the operational record created after a transportation plan has been accepted. It connects the optimized route, participating orders, cargo, vehicle, driver and supporting resources into one traceable movement. From this point onward, the plan is no longer only a calculation — it becomes something that can actually be executed, tracked and completed.
