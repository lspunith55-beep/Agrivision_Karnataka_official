Don't Just Find A Route. Find A Better Journey.

AgriVision's Smart Route system is designed around a simple idea: a vehicle already travelling through an agricultural corridor may have useful unused capacity. Instead of treating every order as an isolated journey, the system can look for compatible orders, evaluate whether they can share the movement and calculate how the resulting transportation cost can be allocated. The objective is not merely to shorten a route. It is to create a more useful movement for the driver, farmer, buyer and logistics network.

AGRIVISION SHARED-LOAD MODEL

One vehicle. Multiple compatible orders. Better utilization.

Consider a simplified example. One accepted order travels from Bangalore to Chitradurga with a standalone transportation value of ₹100. Another compatible order travels from Tumkur to Hiriyur with a standalone value of ₹50. If the vehicle has enough unused capacity and the combined movement is operationally feasible, the Smart Route engine can evaluate a shared journey.

DRIVER DECISION

The final decision belongs to the operational driver.

Smart Route should recommend. It should not blindly force a combination. The driver must be able to understand the proposed movement and either accept the compatible shared load or continue with the original shipment.
