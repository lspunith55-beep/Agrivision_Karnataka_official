FROM DEMAND TO COMMITMENT

A buyer request is more than a message.

Within the AgriVision ecosystem, a buyer's interest should become structured information that can be acted upon. The request identifies the produce, quantity, proposed price, desired date and destination. The Farmer can then evaluate the request and move it into a negotiation rather than losing the opportunity in an unstructured conversation.

📩
01 · REQUEST

Understand demand

Record who wants the produce, what crop is required, how much is needed and where it needs to go.

💬
02 · NEGOTIATION

Reach an agreement

Record the proposed commercial terms and communicate the Farmer's position before the order is finalized.

📦
03 · ORDER

Create commitment

An accepted offer becomes a confirmed order containing the information needed for the next logistics stage.

THE PURPOSE OF THIS SYSTEM

From a simple listing to a meaningful commitment.

Agriculture becomes more connected when information follows the journey of the produce. A listing creates visibility. A buyer request creates demand. Negotiation creates agreement. An order creates commitment. From there, AgriVision can connect the transaction to logistics and financial records — allowing one agricultural journey to remain connected from field to outcome.
