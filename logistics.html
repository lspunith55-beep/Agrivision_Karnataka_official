THE CHALLENGE

Agricultural Movement Is a Coordination Problem

A delivery can involve a farmer, buyer, logistics provider, driver, vehicle, route, consignment and destination. Keeping these moving parts connected is essential.

Fragmented Requests

Transportation requirements can originate from different agricultural transactions and participants.

Timing

Agricultural movement depends on coordinated pickup, transportation and delivery timing.

Multiple Locations

Produce may need to move between farms, collection points, markets and buyer destinations.

Vehicle Coordination

Transportation operations require appropriate vehicles and coordinated driver assignments.

Consignment Visibility

Providers need to understand what is being moved and where the consignment is in its journey.

Delivery Completion

The logistics journey ultimately needs to reach the intended destination.

LOGISTICS WORKFLOW

Receive a transportation or delivery requirement from the agricultural ecosystem.

02 — Understand

Review the produce, locations, timing and transportation needs.

03 — Plan

Organize the movement between pickup and destination.

04 — Assign

Coordinate appropriate transportation resources and drivers.

05 — Move

Execute the agricultural transportation journey.

06 — Complete

Bring the consignment toward successful delivery and completion.
