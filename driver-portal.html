SMART ROUTE

Don't drive empty when a compatible order can share the journey.

AgriVision's Smart Route concept looks beyond individual orders. If a vehicle already travelling from one location to another has available capacity, the system can identify another order whose pickup and delivery pattern is compatible with the existing journey. The objective is to increase vehicle utilization, reduce unnecessary trips and distribute the optimized transportation cost fairly across the participating orders.
