The Right Produce Needs The Right Support

Agricultural procurement does not end when a Buyer finds produce. Depending on the requirement, additional support may be needed for quality, handling, packaging, storage, processing, transportation preparation or other parts of the agricultural journey. The Buyer Services system provides a structured place to discover and record those requirements.
