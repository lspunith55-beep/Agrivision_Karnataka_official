Every Useful Journey Should Create Visible Value.

AgriVision connects completed transportation work with a transparent earnings record. A Driver should be able to understand where an earning came from, which shipment created it, whether Smart Route affected the allocation and what amount is pending or completed. The goal is not simply to show a number. The goal is to make the entire financial journey understandable.

SMART ROUTE ECONOMICS

Shared capacity can create a different economic model.

When compatible orders share one vehicle movement, the system should maintain a transparent record of how the resulting transportation value is allocated. The exact allocation depends on the project's pricing and optimization rules. It should be calculated by the system rather than manually guessed by the Driver.

A Driver should never have to wonder where an earning came from.
