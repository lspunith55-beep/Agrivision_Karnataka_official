THE AGRIVISION LOGISTICS IDEA

A vehicle should not travel with empty space when another compatible order exists.

Agricultural transportation often involves vehicles travelling between different origins and destinations while carrying less than their full useful capacity. AgriVision looks for opportunities to coordinate compatible accepted orders so that available capacity can be used more intelligently. When a shared journey is feasible, the system can calculate the combined transportation requirement and distribute the resulting transportation cost fairly between participating orders.
