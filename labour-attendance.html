WORK PROGRESS

Turn work completed into a measurable record.

Different agricultural jobs need different ways of measuring progress. A daily job may depend primarily on attendance and completion. A quantity-based job may depend on kilograms, crates, bags, rows, acres or another agreed unit. The record should follow the payment model agreed when the job was created.

WORK → EARNINGS

A completed job should create a clear path to your earnings record.

Attendance establishes participation. Work progress records what was completed. Farmer confirmation validates the completion record. The agreed payment model then determines what should move into the earnings and payment workflow. This keeps the operational record connected to the financial record rather than treating them as unrelated systems.
