01 • THE BIGGER PICTURE

Impact begins with connection.

The purpose of AgriVision is not simply to put agricultural information on a screen. It is to create a structure in which the different participants of agriculture can become visible to one another.

01

One ecosystem. Many economic activities.

A farmer may need labour. Labour may need work opportunities. A farm may require equipment. Equipment may require an operator. Produce may require transportation. A buyer may create demand. The value of connection comes from making these relationships easier to coordinate.

09 • INDIA

From the farm to the economy.

Agriculture interacts with services, employment, transportation, trade and markets. A connected agricultural platform should therefore be evaluated through the activity it enables—not by claiming an invented GDP percentage.

01 FARM Production
02 SERVICES Labour • Equipment
03 MOVEMENT Logistics • Drivers
04 MARKET Buyers • Trade
05 ECONOMY Economic activity
AgriVision does not create India's GDP.

Its potential contribution would instead be evaluated through measurable agricultural activity, transactions, service connections, completed operations, logistics movement and participant outcomes.
