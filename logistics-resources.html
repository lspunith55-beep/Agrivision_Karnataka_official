The Right Movement Needs The Right Resources.

Transportation is not only about finding a route or assigning a vehicle. A successful agricultural shipment may depend on a suitable vehicle, an available driver, loading and unloading labour, equipment and specialized operators. This resource layer brings those parts of the AgriVision ecosystem together so that a logistics plan can become a practical, executable movement.

VEHICLE AVAILABILITY

Match cargo with the vehicle that can actually carry it.

Vehicle selection should consider capacity, vehicle type, cargo requirements, route suitability and availability. The largest vehicle is not automatically the best vehicle. A suitable match can reduce unnecessary empty capacity and improve the economics of a movement.
