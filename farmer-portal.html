THE AGRIVISION APPROACH

Agriculture should work as a connected journey

A farmer's work does not stop when a crop is planted. Production leads to harvesting. Harvesting can lead to a marketplace listing. A listing can lead to a buyer request. A buyer request can become a negotiated order. That order may require transportation and finally produce a financial record.

🌱

Record

First, the Farmer creates reliable records about farms, crops and agricultural activity.

🔗

Connect

Those records become useful to the next connected system instead of remaining isolated information.

📈

Grow

The connected journey gives the Farmer a clearer picture of agricultural operations and outcomes.

THE COMPLETE FARMER WORKFLOW

From crop registration to financial outcome

The most important principle of the Farmer module is that a completed operation should create information that can be used by the next appropriate stage.

01

Register the farm and crop

The Farmer first creates the agricultural record. Crop details become the foundation for later marketplace and transaction activities.

02

Make produce available

The Farmer can take an existing crop record into the marketplace and create a structured listing for available produce.

03

Receive buyer demand

A Buyer can discover the produce and submit a request containing requirements such as quantity, proposed price and destination.

04

Negotiate and agree

The Farmer and Buyer can move through the negotiation stage until the relevant commercial details are agreed.

05

Confirm the order

Once an offer is accepted, the transaction becomes a confirmed order containing the information required for the next stage.

06

Connect transport

The confirmed order can provide the basis for a logistics request, connecting pickup, destination, dates and transport requirements.

07

Record the economic result

Income and expenses can then be recorded in the Farmer's finance system, helping connect operational activity with the farm's economic picture.

INFORMATION FLOW

Where does the information go?

AgriVision is designed so that an action does not simply disappear after a form is submitted. The resulting record becomes meaningful to the next connected system.

🌱

Crop → Marketplace

A registered crop provides the agricultural information required to create a marketplace listing.

🛒

Marketplace → Buyer

A marketplace listing becomes discoverable to buyers who are looking for agricultural produce.

💬

Buyer → Negotiation

Buyer requirements can enter the Farmer's transaction workflow and become part of a negotiation.

📦

Order → Logistics

Once the order is confirmed, transport information can be connected to logistics.

🚚

Logistics → Completion

Transport progresses through pickup, movement and delivery stages until completion.

₹

Activity → Finance

Financial records can capture income and expenses associated with agricultural activity.

WHY THE FARMER PORTAL EXISTS

Less fragmentation. More connection.

🧩

Reduce fragmentation

Farm information, market activity, orders and other agricultural activities are designed as parts of one ecosystem.

🔄

Preserve continuity

Information created during one stage can continue into the next stage instead of forcing the Farmer to restart the process.

📊

Build visibility

Activities and completed operations can contribute to the Farmer's overall ecosystem activity.

IMPORTANT

This portal is your map — not the operation desk

The Farmer Portal intentionally does not contain crop-registration forms, marketplace submissions, buyer negotiations, logistics requests or finance-entry forms. Those operations belong inside their dedicated sub-pages. This keeps every system focused, easier to understand and easier to connect.
