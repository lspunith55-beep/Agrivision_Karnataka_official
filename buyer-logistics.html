WHY LOGISTICS MATTERS

The transaction has been agreed. Now the produce has to move.

Agricultural logistics connects the commercial side of a transaction with its physical reality. Produce may need to be collected from a farm, transported through an appropriate route and delivered to the Buyer's specified destination. A clear logistics record helps keep these stages connected to the original order.
