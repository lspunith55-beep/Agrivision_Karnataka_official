SMART ROUTE OPPORTUNITIES

An available vehicle can become an available opportunity.

When Smart Route identifies a potentially compatible movement, the Driver should receive enough information to evaluate it responsibly. The notification should not simply say "new order." It should communicate the relevant operational context, such as the compatible corridor, available capacity, timing, additional movement information and the proposed economic allocation.

SMART ROUTE EXAMPLE

One vehicle. Two compatible orders. One optimized movement.

Consider a vehicle already moving on a Bangalore → Chitradurga corridor with an original transportation value of ₹100. Suppose another compatible order exists from Tumkur → Hiriyur with a value of ₹50, and the vehicle has suitable remaining capacity. If Smart Route determines that the two movements can responsibly share the vehicle, the platform can calculate a transparent allocation according to its pricing rules. For example, if the resulting agreed allocation is ₹65 for one movement and ₹35 for the other, the Driver should see the proposed allocation before accepting the additional movement.

📍 Bangalore → Chitradurga ₹100
📍 Tumkur → Hiriyur ₹50
🧭 Proposed Allocation ₹65 + ₹35

AGRIVISION PRINCIPLE

Information is useful only when it reaches the right person at the right time.

Notifications are therefore not just decorative messages. They are part of the operational workflow. A new shipment can create an opportunity. A route change can require a decision. A payment update can provide clarity. A document warning can prevent a future interruption. A completed delivery can close a movement. The notification center keeps those events visible without forcing the Driver to constantly search through every module.
