MARKETPLACE PHILOSOPHY

Your crop record becomes a market opportunity

A crop registered in the Farmer system should not remain an isolated record. When the Farmer decides that produce is available for sale, the crop can be transformed into a marketplace listing containing the information needed for potential buyers to understand the opportunity.

🌱
STEP 01

Start with a real crop

Marketplace activity begins with agricultural information already recorded in your Farmer crop system.

🏷️
STEP 02

Create a listing

Convert available produce into a structured marketplace listing with quantity, unit, quality and price information.

🤝
STEP 03

Enter the transaction workflow

Buyer interest can move the listing into the Buyer Request and Negotiation stages.
